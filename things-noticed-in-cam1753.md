- **Two different book-name annotations, and telling them apart matters.** Both observed by
  Ben, 2026-08-04.
  - **איכה**, no preceding ספר, above Lam 1:1 on leaf 0105A col 2. This one **does** take up
    body-column space, and marks the start of the book.
  - **ספר איכה**, centered, one instance above leaf 0105B col 2 — whose text starts at
    Lam 1:17, mid-book. This one appears **sporadically** through the book, on no rule Ben
    could determine, and takes **no** body-column space wherever it appears. **What governs
    it is open.** It is neither a start-of-book heading nor a header above every column, and
    an instance says nothing about where a book begins.
- **Lamentations begins on leaf 0105A col 2, nineteen lines up from the bottom** (line 8 of
  26), with Ecclesiastes ending in the same column above the איכה heading. Spread n109,
  fourth column of the spread counting RTL.
- **The lines model that predicted that is worth reusing.** A verse starts a new line and
  takes ceil(atoms / 7.5) of them, 26 text lines to a column, no line charged to any
  annotation. Fitted to Lam 1:17 atop 0105B col 2 and Lam 3:55 atop 0107A col 1, it put
  Lam 1:17–3:54 at 132 lines against the 130 available, then predicted the start of
  Lamentations at 0105A col 2 taking 20 lines — against the 19 Ben then counted. It has now
  been borne out at three places: that start, and Lam 2:3 as the last verse of 0105B col 2.
  The one link in the chain still unchecked is 0105B col 1, predicted to run Lam 1:8–1:16.
  Reading the ספר איכה annotation as a book start, by contrast, would have made Lam 1:1–3:54
  fill five columns at 1048 atoms, 210 per column, against the 145 in the one column measured
  directly (0107A col 1, Lam 3:55 through Lam 4:7). Break overhead cannot close a gap that
  size and runs the wrong way anyway, that stretch having 98 verses to the measured
  column's 19.
