- Leaf 0105B col 2 has a centered ספר איכה above text from Lam 1:17 (Ben, 2026-08-04).
  **That is probably a running header rather than a book-start heading**, and if so the
  manuscript repeats it above each column of the book. Two things point that way. Lam 1:17
  cannot open Lamentations, so a book-start heading would have to sit above the wrong verse.
  And the space works out: taking the heading as a book start makes Lam 1:1–3:54 fill the
  five columns from 0105B col 2 to 0106B col 2, which is 1048 atoms, 210 per column —
  against the 145 atoms in the one column measured directly, 0107A col 1 (Lam 3:55 through
  Lam 4:7, per the page index). Break overhead cannot close a gap that size and runs the
  wrong way anyway, that stretch having 98 verses to the measured column's 19. Starting the
  five columns at Lam 1:17 instead reconciles them: a lines model in which a verse starts a
  new line and takes ceil(atoms / 7.5) of them puts that stretch at 132 lines against the
  130 available, and the book-start reading at 148. **Unverified** — nobody has yet looked
  at whether the header repeats above other columns.
